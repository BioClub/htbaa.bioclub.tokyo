# HTBAA 2026

Welcome to the HTBAA@BioClub Site! [BioClub Tokyo](https://bioclub.tokyo) is one of the Global Nodes of the growing HTGAA/HTBAA Network.

Lectures and Recitations are shared globally from the MIT Course 'How to Grow Almost Anything', each node will organize additional Class & Homework Reviews sessions, as well as hands-on experiments in the lab of the local node.

# Committed Listeners & Global Listeners

- The course can be taken in two modes. Either as a *Global Listener* to audit the course, or as a *Committed Listener*. Committed Listeners commit to watch the weekly lectures and recitations, do the weekly homework on time and actively participate in the local node (in this case: BioClub) meetings.

- Being a *Committed Listener* is hard. It will take time watch the lectures and join the meetings.

# Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does it cost to participate in HTBAA?
The global costs for HTBAA is free, indiviual node might charge for local costs.

### Can I get a certificate for completing the course?
HTBAA does not issue certificates at this point. Your documentation pages and the listing of your name on the node and global HTBAA site will be proof for your participation.
